Our programs

Two programs. One regional commitment.

Face to face

Ararat Community Learning

Free face to face education and consultation

Country Therapy is partnering with Ararat Neighbourhood House to provide free community education and consultation every two months. Sessions will be delivered by members of our occupational therapy and management team and are designed for parents, carers, grandparents, educators, farming families and other community members who want practical information they can use in everyday life.

  • Delivered by our occupational therapy and management team
  • Held every two months at Ararat Neighbourhood House
  • Open to parents, carers, grandparents and educators
  • Time set aside for questions and practical discussion

Online

EmpowerEd

Free online support for regional educators

EmpowerEd is Country Therapy's free online learning program for teachers and education staff across Western rural and regional Victoria. Ten targeted topic areas can be completed in the order that best matches a classroom's current needs, helping educators build practical skills for supporting students living with disability.

  • Ten targeted topic areas
  • Complete them in whatever order the classroom needs
  • Built for access across rural and regional Victoria
  • Practical strategies that work in real classrooms

What sessions cover

What people can learn about

Sessions may cover autism, ADHD, intellectual disability, developmental delay, sensory processing, emotional regulation, school participation, routines and other neurodevelopmental differences. Each session will leave time for questions and practical discussion.

Autism ADHD Intellectual disability Developmental delay Sensory processing Emotional regulation School participation Routines and daily life

Regional focus: Ararat, Pyrenees, Grampians, Wimmera and surrounding communities.

Why we do it

Our aim is simple: bring useful information closer to the people who need it. For families living on farms, in small towns and across the wider Ararat, Pyrenees, Grampians and Wimmera regions, distance should not be the reason they miss out on early information and practical support.

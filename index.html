About Us

Small team.
Big impact.

We are a family-centred, client-centred mobile occupational therapy practice. Since our founding, we have worked with clients throughout the Ararat, Horsham, Melton and Melbourne regions — always prioritising the individuality of each person we serve.

We believe in not just working with our clients but empowering them to understand and achieve their best potential — wherever they call home.

How It Works

Getting started is simple.

Four clear steps from first contact to ongoing support. We make the process as smooth as possible.

01

Get in Touch

Call, email or complete our online enquiry. We'll respond within one business day to discuss your needs.

02

Book an Assessment

We schedule an initial visit at your home, school or community setting — wherever feels most natural for you.

03

Receive Your Plan

04

Ongoing sessions at a pace that suits you — with regular reviews to track progress and adjust goals over time.

Assessments

Assessments we offer

Evidence-based assessments across the full lifespan — from children to older adults.

Paediatric

Developmental Assessment

Evaluates a child's fine motor, gross motor, sensory processing and daily living skills relative to developmental milestones.

Paediatric

Sensory Processing Assessment

Identifies how a child processes sensory information to support participation at home, school and in the community.

Adult

Functional Capacity Evaluation

Assesses an individual's ability to perform daily tasks, used for NDIS, legal and workplace purposes.

Adult

Home Safety Assessment

Evaluates the safety of a person's home environment and recommends modifications or equipment to support independent living.

Mental Health

Mental Health OT Assessment

Supports adults, youth and children experiencing mental health challenges to improve daily functioning and quality of life.

Adult

Cognitive Assessment

Evaluates cognitive function including memory, attention and problem-solving to understand impact on daily activities.

National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS)

Country Therapy is a registered NDIS provider delivering mobile occupational therapy across regional and metropolitan Victoria. We support participants of all ages — from early childhood through to older adults — helping them build skills, increase independence and participate more fully in everyday life.

We work closely with participants, families, support coordinators and other providers under both Plan-Managed and Self-Managed NDIS plans. Every therapy plan is goal-oriented, evidence-based and tailored to the individual.

  • Fine & Gross Motor Development
  • Assistive Technology Assessments & Recommendations
  • Home Modifications & Equipment Prescription

Medicare-Funded Occupational Therapy

Country Therapy offers high-quality mobile occupational therapy with a Medicare rebate, delivered in your home, school, workplace or community setting across regional and metro Victoria.

If you or your child need support with developmental delays, mental health, injury recovery, disability, chronic illness or functional independence, you may be eligible for Medicare-funded OT under a Chronic Disease Management (CDM) Plan. Speak with your GP and ask for a Team Care Arrangement (TCA) and GP Management Plan (GPMP) referral — this allows up to five allied health sessions per calendar year with a Medicare rebate.

  • Paediatric and adult occupational therapy
  • Functional assessments and capacity building
  • Sensory and emotional regulation support
  • Fine and gross motor skills development
  • Mental health support under Better Access (where eligible)

Ask your GP today if you're eligible for a Medicare referral to Country Therapy.

How It Works

What does mobile OT actually mean?

Unlike clinic-based therapy, we come to the environment that matters most — the place where real life happens.

01

At Your Home

We assess and treat in your actual living environment — meaning our recommendations are practical, realistic and immediately applicable.

02

At Your School

For children and young people, we work within their school or early learning environment — collaborating directly with teachers and support staff.

03

In Your Community

Community venues, day programs, supported accommodation — wherever you spend your time, we can meet you there and support your goals in context.

04

Via Telehealth

For clients in more remote areas or those with transport barriers, we also offer telehealth sessions to ensure access to quality OT regardless of location.

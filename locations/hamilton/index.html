FAQs about OT in Hamilton

Do you offer occupational therapy in Hamilton?

Yes — Country Therapy provides regular mobile OT visits to Hamilton and the wider Southern Grampians region. We come to you at home, school or in the community, so there's no need to travel to a clinic.

Do you travel to small towns near Hamilton?

Yes — we cover the surrounding areas including Coleraine, Casterton, Dunkeld, Penshurst, Macarthur and more. If you're unsure whether we reach your location, just call or email and we'll confirm right away.

Do you provide aged care OT in Hamilton?

Yes. We work with aged care providers and individual clients in Hamilton for in-home and facility-based OT — including falls prevention, equipment prescription, pressure care and dementia support.

Can my local GP refer me to Country Therapy?

Yes. Ask your Hamilton GP for a GP Management Plan (GPMP) and Team Care Arrangement (TCA) to access up to 5 OT sessions per year under Medicare with a partial rebate.

Do you support NDIS participants in Hamilton?

Yes — plan-managed, self-managed and agency-managed NDIS participants of all ages are welcome. No specialist referral is needed for NDIS services.

How quickly can I get an appointment in Hamilton?

We respond to all enquiries within one business day. Contact us today and we'll find the next available appointment in the Southern Grampians schedule.

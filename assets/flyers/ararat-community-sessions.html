Free education and consultation, every two months

Country Therapy and Ararat Neighbourhood House are partnering to bring practical, trusted information to local families and community members, right here in Ararat. Sessions are delivered by our occupational therapy and management team, with time set aside for questions.

Come along to learn and ask questions about:
Autism
ADHD
Intellectual disability
Developmental delay
Sensory and emotional regulation
School and everyday participation

For parents, carers, grandparents, educators, farming families and anyone in the community who wants to understand more.

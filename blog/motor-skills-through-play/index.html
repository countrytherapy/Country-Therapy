Developing motor skills through play

As a parent or caregiver, you play a vital role in helping children develop essential motor skills through play.

On this page

  1. Gross motor skills
  2. Fine motor skills
  3. Sensory play
  4. Additional tips

Motor skills are crucial for kids to perform various physical tasks, maintain good posture, and enhance overall coordination. In this article, we'll delve into exciting games and activities that enhance gross and fine motor skills, including balance exercises, obstacle courses, and finger painting.

Gross motor skills: the foundation of physical movement

Gross motor skills involve large muscle groups that enable kids to move, jump, and maintain balance. Here are some engaging activities to enhance gross motor skills:

Obstacle course

Set up an obstacle course using household items, such as couch cushions, chairs, and blankets. Kids can crawl, jump, and climb through the course, developing their balance and coordination.

Balance beams

Create a balance beam using a broom handle or a narrow board. Kids can walk along the beam, improving their balance and stability.

Ball games

Play catch, roll, or toss balls of varying sizes and textures. This activity enhances hand eye coordination and overall gross motor skills.

Simon Says

A classic game that encourages kids to move their bodies in different ways, promoting gross motor skills and self regulation.

Freeze dance

Put on some music and have kids dance, then freeze in place when the music stops. This activity develops gross motor skills and self control.

Fine motor skills: the precision of movement

Fine motor skills involve smaller muscle groups that enable kids to perform precise movements, such as drawing, writing, and using small objects. Here are some engaging activities to enhance fine motor skills:

Finger painting

Set up a space where kids can paint using their fingers, exploring different colours and textures. This activity develops finger strength and dexterity.

Playdough creations

Make or buy playdough and let kids shape, mould, and create various objects. This activity enhances hand eye coordination and fine motor skills.

Puzzles and play

Engage kids with puzzles, LEGO, or building blocks, which develop problem solving skills and fine motor dexterity.

Colouring and drawing

Provide various materials, such as crayons, markers, and paper, and encourage kids to express their creativity, developing fine motor skills and hand eye coordination.

Dressing and undressing

Encourage kids to dress and undress themselves, developing fine motor skills and independence.

Sensory play: exploring the world

Sensory play is essential for kids to develop their senses and motor skills. Here are some activities to incorporate:

  • Sensory bin. Fill a bin with rice, beans, or sand and hide small toys or other objects for kids to find and explore.
  • Play with play balls. Fill a container with play balls and let kids explore, developing their sense of touch and fine motor skills.
  • Finger play. Fill a container with water and let kids play with their fingers, exploring the sensation of water and developing fine motor skills.

Additional tips

Keep in mind

  • Encourage kids to engage in outdoor play, exploring nature and developing motor skills through physical activity.
  • Adapt activities to suit individual needs and abilities, ensuring inclusivity and fun for all.
  • Make playtime a family affair, bonding with kids while promoting healthy development.
  • Provide a safe and supportive environment for kids to explore and develop their motor skills.

By incorporating these activities into your child's daily routine, you'll be supporting their overall motor skill development and setting them up for a lifetime of physical confidence and coordination, while also fostering creativity, problem solving skills, and a strong foundation for future academic and sporting success.
